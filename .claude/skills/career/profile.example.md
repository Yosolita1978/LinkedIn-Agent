# Career profile (example)

Copy this file to `profile.local.md` in the same folder and fill in your own facts.
`profile.local.md` is gitignored, so your personal details stay out of the repo.
The /career skill reads it before judging jobs or posts.

- **Location:** <city> (<time zone>).
- **Work authorization:** <e.g. US citizen / permanent resident / visa type>. <Clearance: yes/no>.
  Postings that name an eligibility group you belong to are a **plus**; postings that
  require one you don't meet are a skip.
- **Experience:** about <N> years. Never claim more. Skip at <N+2>+ years required; review at <N>–<N+1>.
- **Teaching / audience:** <e.g. adult learners, not K-12>.
- **Onsite / hybrid OK in:** <cities>.
- **Pay floor:** about $<amount>. Stated pay below it → review.
